Moss in the browser

Downloading the page
Moss runs completely on your own GPU with WebGPU. The models are downloaded once from Hugging Face as heavily compressed int4 Convrot .safetensors (Moss 3 GB, text encoder 4.3 GB) and kept in your browser. A browser page can only use about 4 GB, so the quality is lower than with int8 in the desktop version. Tested in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on the desktop and Chrome on Android phones, all with WebGPU enabled; the GPU needs 4 GB of memory or more. WebGPU limits the speed, for really fast image generation use the desktop version. You can add any moss lora (e.g. from civitai.com or huggingface.com) on top or even import your whole ComfyUI workflow here. Your images never leave your device, they are only kept in this browser's storage together with any models and loras, next time you visit everything is still here till you kill your browser cache. For the visit counter and sampling time of each image it calls stats/, you can download this site and run it completely locally yourself as well.
